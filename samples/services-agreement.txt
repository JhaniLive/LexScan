SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on 3 March 2025 between:

(1) NORTHWIND LTD, a company registered in England (No. 09876543) whose
    registered office is at 14 Bishopsgate, London EC2N 3AR ("Client"); and
(2) ORBIT STUDIO PRIVATE LIMITED, a company registered in India (CIN
    U72900MH2019PTC123456) whose office is at Andheri East, Mumbai 400069
    ("Supplier").

1. SERVICES
1.1 The Supplier shall provide website design, hosting and maintenance services
    as described in Schedule 1 (the "Services").
1.2 The Client may, by written notice, vary the scope of the Services. The
    Supplier shall implement any such variation without additional charge where
    the variation does not increase effort by more than 20%.

2. TERM AND RENEWAL
2.1 This Agreement commences on 1 April 2025 and continues for an initial term
    of twelve (12) months.
2.2 It shall renew automatically for successive twelve (12) month terms unless
    either party gives written notice of non-renewal not less than ninety (90)
    days before the end of the then-current term.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT
3.1 The Client shall pay INR 2,00,000 per month, invoiced monthly in arrears.
3.2 Invoices are payable within fifteen (15) days of receipt.
3.3 Late payment carries interest at 18% per annum, accruing daily.
3.4 Fees may be increased by the Supplier once per year on thirty (30) days
    notice, by no more than 10%.

4. TERMINATION
4.1 The Client may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time on
    thirty (30) days written notice.
4.2 The Supplier may terminate only where the Client commits a material breach
    which remains uncured sixty (60) days after written notice.
4.3 On termination, the Client shall pay for Services performed up to the
    termination date. No refund of prepaid fees shall be made.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
5.1 All intellectual property created by the Supplier in performing the
    Services, together with all pre-existing tools, libraries and know-how
    incorporated into the deliverables, shall vest in the Client absolutely on
    creation.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY
6.1 Each party shall keep the other's confidential information secret for a
    period of three (3) years from disclosure.

7. LIABILITY
7.1 The Supplier's total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not
    exceed one (1) month's fees.
7.2 The Client's liability under this Agreement is not limited.
7.3 Neither party excludes liability for death, personal injury or fraud.

8. INDEMNITY
8.1 The Supplier shall indemnify and hold harmless the Client against all
    claims, losses, damages and costs arising out of or in connection with the
    Services, howsoever caused.

9. NON-SOLICITATION
9.1 During the term and for twenty-four (24) months afterwards, the Supplier
    shall not employ or engage any person employed by the Client.

10. ASSIGNMENT
10.1 The Client may assign this Agreement freely. The Supplier may not assign
     or subcontract without the Client's prior written consent.

11. NOTICES
11.1 Notices shall be in writing and sent to the registered office of the
     receiving party. Email is not valid service.

12. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION
12.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of India.
12.2 The courts at Mumbai shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
