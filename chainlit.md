# LexScan

Legal document review and situation help for India.

**Upload a document** — contract, NDA, lease, notice, court paper. `.pdf`,
`.docx`, `.txt`, or a photo of the pages. You get a summary, the clauses that
matter, every deadline, a risk register and a negotiation brief. Then ask
questions and get answers with the clause quoted.

**Or tell me what happened** — describe the problem in your own words, including
which state you're in. You get the law that applies, what to do next and with
whom, the deadlines, and the evidence to keep — plus the questions worth
answering to sharpen it.

**Then get the paperwork** — `/draft police-complaint`, `/draft legal-notice`,
`/draft statement`, `/draft consumer-complaint`, `/draft rti` and more. Written
to be copied, filled in and sent.

**Check what's current** — `/news <topic>` searches the live web, because a
model's training is always older than the news.

---

*LexScan is an AI legal assistant, not a law firm. It gives legal information,
not legal advice, and no lawyer-client relationship is created. Section numbers
and state rules change — verify anything you rely on, and take anything serious
to a qualified lawyer.*
